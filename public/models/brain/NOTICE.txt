Changes made for Hearingpedia:
- Converted GIFTI arrays to JSON; rounded coordinates to 0.1 mm and sulcal values to 0.01.
- Display rotates and uniformly scales both hemispheres, with a small medial gap.
- Sulcal values provide subtle shading; nodes are distributed for navigation.
- This is a cortical surface template; it does not include cerebellum or brainstem.
- Knowledge node positions are not functional or anatomical localizations.

Conversion script: scripts/prepare-brain-model.py
